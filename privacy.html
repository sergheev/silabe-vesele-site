Privacy Policy

October 27, 2026

In short: the game doesn't collect any information about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, ads or analytics, and the game doesn't send anything over the internet.

The rest of the words unlock with a one-time App Store purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see or keep card details. A grown-up has to solve a small sum before paying.

The game is made for kids and follows Apple's rules for Kids category apps. We don't ask children for any information.
